innate vs. adaptive immunity two arms of the immune system

Innate immunity non‑specific

First line of defense. Rapid response (minutes to hours). No immunological memory. Recognizes broad pathogen‑associated molecular patterns (PAMPs) via pattern‑recognition receptors (PRRs).
fastno memorygermline‑encodedPRRsphysical barriers
includes: skin, mucosa, phagocytes, NK cells, complement

Adaptive immunity specific

Slower onset (days to weeks), but highly specific. Generates immunological memory. Relies on B cells (antibodies) and T cells (cellular immunity). Clonal selection and expansion.
memoryspecificitysomatic recombinationclonal expansionMHC restriction
includes: humoral (B cells) & cell‑mediated (T cells)
